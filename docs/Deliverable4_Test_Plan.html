    • Objective

    Purpose

    This document describes the plan for testing the prototype of the G-TRAMS (Gasan Tricycle Records and Application Management System) developed for the Office of the Municipal Vice Mayor in the Municipality of Gasan, Marinduque. This Test Plan supports the following objectives:

  • Determine the existing project and the software features that should be tested;
  • Identify the required testing requirements and operational workflows across the 8 core modules;
  • Determine and describe the test strategies to be performed (functional, integration, performance, compatibility, and UAT);
  • Identify required resources, test environments, and tools that will be used for testing; and
  • Determine deliverables and documentation of the test activities prior to final municipal deployment.

    • Scope

    G-TRAMS (Gasan Tricycle Records and Application Management System) is a web-based management system designed to streamline tricycle franchise applications, records monitoring, association roster tracking, cashier payment verification, and regulatory enforcement for the Office of the Municipal Vice Mayor (OVM) of Gasan, Marinduque.

    These are the following functional specifications of G-TRAMS:

    1. User Authentication and Registration;

    2. Manage System Settings;

    3. Manage Account Profile;

    4. Submit TODA Records;

    5. Apply and Renew Franchise Applications;

    6. Franchise Payment and Release Verification;

    7. Generate System Reports; and

    8. Manage Franchise Cancellation and Revocation.

    Feature Functionality

    Description of the Functions of the System

    User Authentication and Registration – G-TRAMS is capable of providing secure, role-based access for various system actors including System Administrators, OVM Staff, Tricycle Operators, and TODA Presidents. The system allows tricycle operators to register a new user account by submitting their demographic credentials and contact details, with mobile OTP verification to activate the account.

    Manage System Settings – System settings management enables authorized administrators to configure operational parameters essential for municipal tricycle governance in Gasan. The system allows administrators to define the active fiscal year, set municipal franchise fee amounts, configure base fare matrices, and strictly enforce the maximum quota of allowed tricycle units per operator (maximum of 2 units). It also includes administrative controls for user account activation and deactivation.

    Manage Account Profile – Account profile management allows registered operators and users to manage their personal credentials and contact records. The system allows operators to update their personal contact details and residential address, as well as securely change account passwords through verification of current credentials.

    Submit TODA Records – TODA records submission enables TODA Presidents to submit official membership rosters and driver directories to the Office of the Municipal Vice Mayor. The system provides tools for System Admin and Staff to review, validate, and update submitted TODA records to verify active association standing and route legitimacy before franchise issuance.

    Apply and Renew Franchise Applications – Franchise application management is the primary operational function of G-TRAMS. The system allows tricycle operators to submit new applications or renewals along with digital copies of required supporting documents (OR/CR, Driver's License, Cedula, TODA Endorsement, and Barangay Clearance). The system enables System Admin and Staff to evaluate, approve, reject, or return applications for compliance, while providing operators with real-time tracking of their application status and history. Managing franchise applications includes the following specific features:

  • Apply New Franchise
  • Renew Franchise Application
  • Upload Supporting Documents
  • Evaluate and Verify Applications
  • Return Application for Compliance
  • Approve or Reject Application
  • Real-Time Application Tracking

    • Franchise Payment and Release Verification – The franchise payment and release verification module manages the transition of approved applications towards official permit release. Once an application is approved and marked as "Ready for Pickup", the system notifies the operator to proceed to the Municipal Cashier at the Municipal Hall to pay the prescribed franchise fee. The system displays the applicant's reference number, payable fee details (₱500), and municipal instructions, and allows System Admin and Staff to record the Official Receipt (OR) Number to confirm payment before releasing the permit and activating the franchise. Managing franchise payment and release verification includes the following specific features:

  • Notify Operator to Proceed to Municipal Cashier
  • View Payment Details and Reference Number
  • Record Official Receipt (OR) Number (Admin/Staff)
  • Payment Verification and Franchise Activation

    • Generate System Reports – Reporting capabilities enable municipal administrators to generate, filter, and export operational records for administrative monitoring and decision-making. The system is capable of filtering the municipal franchise master list, monitoring renewal lists, identifying expired franchises, auditing collection totals, displaying summary counts, and exporting reports into downloadable CSV and printable PDF formats.

    Manage Franchise Cancellation and Revocation – Franchise cancellation and revocation management allows administrators to handle voluntary cancellations and record operator violations as legal grounds for franchise revocation. The system automatically updates the franchise status to "Cancelled" or "Revoked" upon administrative action, notifies affected operators, and allows them to view the official reason or violation records through their portal. Managing franchise cancellation and revocation includes the following specific features:

  • Voluntary Franchise Cancellation
  • Record Operator Violations
  • Franchise Revocation Processing
  • Automatic Status Updating
  • Operator Notification and Violation History Viewing

    • Business Rules

    FunctionsBusiness Rules
    **User Authentication and Registration****[BR1]** The system must provide a form to capture user registration details.
    **[BR2]** The system should not accept duplicate operator accounts with the same email or contact number.
    **[BR3]** Password entries must be securely encrypted using bcrypt before database storage.
    **[BR4]** The system must restrict portal navigation strictly according to user roles (Admin, Staff, Operator, TODA President).
    **Manage System Settings****[BR5]** Only authorized System Administrators can configure the active fiscal year, unit quotas, and franchise fees.
    **[BR6]** An individual operator cannot register or own more than two (2) active tricycle units under a single name.
    **[BR7]** Deactivated user accounts must be prevented from logging in or filing new applications.
    **Manage Account Profile****[BR8]** The system must allow operators to edit their contact details and residential address.
    **[BR9]** Password updates require verification of the existing password before changes are committed.
    **Submit TODA Records****[BR10]** Only recognized TODA Presidents can submit official association membership rosters (.pdf/.xlsx).
    **[BR11]** Admin/Staff must validate that an applicant is listed in the official TODA roster before approving a franchise application.
    **Apply and Renew Franchise Applications****[BR12]** Mandatory requirements (OR/CR, Driver's License, TODA Certificate, Barangay Clearance, Cedula) must be uploaded before application submission.
    **[BR13]** Renewals must pre-populate existing operator and vehicle details to avoid redundant data entry.
    **[BR14]** Returned applications must provide corrective remarks explaining deficient or unreadable documents.
    **Franchise Payment & Release Verification****[BR15]** The system must automatically notify approved operators to settle franchise fees at the Municipal Cashier upon reaching "Ready for Pickup" status.
    **[BR16]** The system must display the applicant's reference number and payable fee amount for presentation to the Municipal Cashier.
    **[BR17]** System Admin/Staff must record a valid Official Receipt (OR) Number to verify cashier payment before updating the franchise status to "Active".
    **Generate System Reports****[BR18]** The system must be able to generate and filter operational masterlists, renewal lists, expired franchise lists, and collection summaries.
    **[BR19]** The system must be able to export reports to downloadable CSV and printable PDF formats.
    **Manage Franchise Cancellation & Revocation****[BR20]** Operators can only voluntarily cancel applications that are in Pending or unreleased status.
    **[BR21]** Admin/Staff can revoke an active franchise based on codified municipal violations (fare overcharging, out-of-line operation, reckless driving, expired credentials).
    **[BR22]** Franchise status must automatically update to "Revoked" or "Cancelled", notifying the operator with official violation grounds.

    Test Strategy

    Application Name

    G-TRAMS (Gasan Tricycle Records and Application Management System): A Web-Based Tricycle Franchise Management and Records Verification System for the Municipality of Gasan, Marinduque.

    Test Approach

    Validation testing will be done using a set of test cases designed specifically for G-TRAMS. Validation will be implemented in order to determine whether the developed system meets all municipal and user requirements and whether its specifications are correct.

    As a testing technique, Black Box Testing will be conducted. Black Box testing will be performed without looking at the internal code structure, implementation details, and internal paths of the system. Functionality testing as a type of black box testing will be performed across all 8 functional modules. System and Integration Testing will also be conducted to ensure that all modules work seamlessly together.

    Features to be Tested

    The following are the features and functions to be tested:

  • User Authentication and Registration
  • Manage System Settings (Fee configuration, 2-unit limit, user account activation/deactivation)
  • Manage Account Profile (Contact info, password change)
  • Submit TODA Records (Roster upload, Admin validation)
  • Apply and Renew Franchise Applications (New application, fast-track renewal, document uploads, review workflow)
  • Franchise Payment and Release Verification (Cashier notification, fee presentation, OR Number recording, status activation)
  • Generate System Reports (Masterlist, expired franchise filtering, CSV/PDF export)
  • Manage Franchise Cancellation and Revocation (Voluntary cancel, violation recording, revocation)

    • Features Not to be Tested

    The following are the features and functions that will not be tested due to external dependencies:

  • Physical printing hardware communications (printer mechanical errors).
  • Direct electronic payment gateway integrations (payments are transacted in-person at the Municipal Cashier window).

    Regression Testing

    Regression testing will be executed whenever code modifications, bug fixes, or system adjustments are made to G-TRAMS. This ensures that modifications—such as updating fee structures, route zoning, or quota enforcement—do not negatively impact or break existing features such as operator registration, document uploads, or real-time application status tracking.

    User Acceptance Testing

    User Acceptance Testing (UAT) will be performed by the project proponents, municipal staff, and representative tricycle operators to verify and accept the system before final deployment. UAT will be conducted as the final phase of testing after functional, integration, and system testing is completed.

    In a UAT, the following are the conditions that must be first met before the test is conducted:

  • Business Requirements must be available and fully approved;
  • Application Code should be fully developed and deployed;
  • Unit Testing, Integration Testing & System Testing should be completed;
  • No Showstoppers, High, or Medium defects in System Integration Test Phase;
  • Only Cosmetic / minor styling errors are acceptable before UAT;
  • Regression Testing should be completed with no major defects;
  • All reported defects should be fixed and tested before UAT;
  • Traceability matrix for all testing should be completed;
  • UAT Environment must be ready; and
  • Sign-off communication from System Testing Team that the system is ready for UAT execution.

    ##### Software Quality Evaluation

Its criteria will be based on the ISO 25010 Software Quality Model. This software evaluation will assess criteria such as functionality, usability, reliability, performance efficiency, compatibility, security, maintainability, and portability, which will be rated using the Likert Scale rating basis.     • RISK Management

    Risk during system testing can be present, that is why risk management is important to carry out testing or to design and execute the scenarios, such that the top business risks which will have a negative impact on the business as identified by the customer are unearthed in their product or feature early in the life cycle and are mitigated by implementing mitigation measures.

    To ensure proper risk management during testing, the following risks are identified together with its mitigation plan that will help solve the possible risk that may arise.

    RiskMitigation Plan
    **SCHEDULE:** Testing schedule is tight. If the start of the testing is delayed due to design tasks, the test cannot be extended beyond the UAT scheduled start date.• The testing team can control the preparation tasks (in advance) and the early communication with involved parties.
    • Buffer days have been added to the schedule for contingencies.
    **RESOURCES:** Not enough resources, resources onboarding too late.• Holidays and schedules have been estimated and built into the plan; tasks are distributed evenly across team members.
    **DEFECTS:** Defects are found at a late stage of the cycle; defects discovered late are most likely due to unclear specifications and are time-consuming to resolve.• Defect management plan is in place to ensure prompt communication, reproduction, and fixing of issues.
    **SCOPE:** Scope changes or functionality is not yet finalized.• Scope is well defined and strictly bounded to the 8 official modules approved for G-TRAMS.
    **Natural Disasters & Network Outages:** Power or internet interruptions in the area.• Repositories and cloud databases (Render, Atlas, Cloudinary) are remotely hosted; mobile hotspots and offline copies are maintained.
    **Non-availability of Independent Test Environment and Accessibility:** Hosting downtime impacting testing schedule.• Staging environment on Render and Vercel is monitored; local testing setups are maintained as contingency.
    **Delayed Testing Due To New Issues:** New defects identified during testing becoming showstoppers.• If new defects are discovered, defect management and issue escalation procedures are in place to immediately provide a resolution.
