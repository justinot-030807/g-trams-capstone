# Batch 2 Treasury Settlement

- H6 reserves payment on the franchise, appends a durable ledger entry, then publishes Paid and For Signing. Matching OR retries resume interrupted work and preserve issued-permit discipline.
- M6 allocates MTOP numbers through an Asia/Manila year counter seeded from existing records. Cancellation DOCX exports use the stored MTOP number.
- `batch2H6M6TreasurySettlement.test.js` covers failure recovery, receipt concurrency, discipline, number allocation, and DOCX output.
